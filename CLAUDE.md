# GTM Library

My public portfolio of GTM engineering use cases: case studies, skills, plugins, digital products and decks.

## Who I am
- GTM Lead in high-growth tech businesses, across marketing and sales, B2B and D2C.
- Mission: automate my own job and make myself redundant.
- This repo is my public build log. Use cases grow as I go; each ships as a case study, a skill or plugin, a pitch and a post.
- Readers:
  - Entrepreneurs who can't afford senior GTM hires but need automated systems to build and scale.
  - GTM professionals, employed or between roles, who want to upskill in AI.
  - Anyone who wants proof you can learn AI by building.
- Voice: plain English, first person, short sentences. Show the work and the results, no hype.

## Safety rules — this repo is public
1. Never write API keys, tokens or passwords into any file except .env.
2. Before every commit, check the staged files for secrets, emails, phone numbers or client/prospect names outside /data. If you find any, stop and tell me.
3. If I paste a key into the chat, put it in .env and remind me why.
4. Case studies use "Target company" instead of the real name until I say it's pitched.

## How case studies are written
- Every case study lives in its use-case folder's README.md, under "## Case study".
- Open with a one-line headline that leads with the business outcome and its KPI (e.g. "Cut lead research from 4 hours to 10 minutes per account").
- Then, always in this order:
  1. **Problem:** the GTM pain, in one or two sentences a non-technical reader gets.
  2. **Build:** what I built, the tools, and the key steps. Link the skill or plugin.
  3. **Result:** quantified with business KPIs where possible (pipeline, conversion rate, cost per lead, hours saved, revenue). Show before/after.
  4. **Lesson:** what I learned and what I'd do differently.
- Length: a 3-minute read, so it can be shared on social media as is.
- Say which reader it helps most (entrepreneur, GTM professional, learner) and write for them.
- Company names: see safety rule 4.
- Never invent results. If there's no proof yet, write "Result: pending".

## Naming rules
- Use-case folders: two-digit number + kebab-case, e.g. `use-cases/02-geo-audit-ai-visibility/`. Next use case takes the next free number.
- Skills and plugins: kebab-case, verb first, says what it does, e.g. `skills/score-icp-accounts/`.
- Files: lowercase-with-dashes. Every folder has a README.md.
- Commit messages: short, present tense, says what changed, e.g. `Add case study for ICP scoring`.
